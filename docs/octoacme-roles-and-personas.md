# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test plan development, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure features meet quality standards before release and work closely with developers and product teams to define testability requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Design test scenarios covering happy paths, edge cases, and error conditions
- Execute manual and automated testing throughout the project lifecycle
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Validate fixes and perform regression testing
- Advise on testability and quality metrics
- Participate in estimation and planning for quality considerations

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce defect escape rate to production
- Enable continuous delivery with confidence
- Shift-left quality through early test planning

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with product and development teams
- Daily stand-ups for in-flight defect tracking
- Weekly QA sync with PM on quality metrics and blockers
- Sprint retrospectives to improve testing efficiency

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test case design, acceptance criteria clarity, and testability implementation
- **With Product Managers**: Validate feature acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate quality risks, testing timelines, and blockers

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction, review architectural decisions, and mentor developers. They ensure technical quality, scalability, and maintainability of the solution.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architectural decisions
- Review design proposals and code for technical excellence
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Ensure consistency across the codebase
- Participate in capacity planning and estimation

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing
- Enable sustainable development velocity

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical spike discussions
- Code review guidance and mentoring
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical blocker escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide technical guidance, review code, and mentor on patterns
- **With Product Managers**: Translate technical constraints into product trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate technical risks and capacity implications

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers ensure security requirements are met, perform security reviews, and identify risks. They embed security into the development process and support compliance efforts.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Perform security design reviews and threat modeling
- Conduct security testing and code reviews for vulnerabilities
- Monitor security metrics and incident response
- Advise on secure coding practices and libraries
- Support compliance and audit activities
- Escalate security incidents according to established protocols

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and breaches
- Build security into the development process
- Maintain compliance with regulatory requirements
- Enable rapid, secure feature delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews during planning
- Security checkpoint in PR reviews
- Weekly security sync with development leads
- Incident response and post-incident reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Review code for vulnerabilities, advise on secure patterns
- **With Product Managers**: Define security requirements and scope implications
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate security risks and mitigation timelines

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and decision-making authority. They ensure projects align with organizational priorities and remove high-level blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Review and approve project scope and major changes
- Support resource allocation and priority arbitration
- Remove organizational and political blockers
- Review project status and key decisions
- Communicate outcomes to broader leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable smooth project execution through timely decisions
- Build stakeholder confidence and support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder briefings and reviews
- Approval gates for scope changes and major decisions
- Escalation channel for blockers and strategic issues
- Executive status reports and outcome reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Provide strategic direction and decision-making authority
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business priorities and success metrics
- **With Developers**: Provide context for "why" behind requirements

---

## Support / Operations Lead

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Leads represent end-user perspectives and operational considerations. They ensure products are maintainable, supportable, and operationally sound.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate customer feedback and pain points
- Advise on operability, monitoring, and maintainability
- Define operational requirements and runbooks
- Identify gaps between feature design and operational reality
- Support release preparation and post-deployment verification
- Represent operational constraints in planning

### Goals
- Ensure products are easy to support and maintain
- Reduce operational incidents and support burden
- Enable sustainable operations and customer satisfaction
- Build strong feedback loop between customers and product

### Typical Communication
- Operational requirements review during planning
- Feedback sessions with product and development teams
- Release readiness reviews and runbook validation
- Post-release operational sync

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Advise on monitoring, logging, and operational patterns
- **With Product Managers**: Provide customer feedback and operational constraints
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate operational readiness and support considerations

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Cross-functional collaboration between all personas ensures comprehensive project delivery and reduces accountability gaps.
